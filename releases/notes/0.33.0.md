## 亮点

- 新增独立外观设置：在「设置 → 通用 → 外观」选择跟随系统、浅色或深色，设置窗口与悬浮面板随之调整；外观选择独立于体验主题和声音设置，并在重启后保留（#324）

## 修复

- 修复浅色外观下展开的工具结果难以阅读的问题，覆盖代码、Shell 输出、文件名、行号、待办和原始结果；Diff 增删文字也采用适合浅色背景的颜色
- 修复 Codex 会话恢复处理或完成后，绿色铃铛与旧提示卡仍持续占用会话入口的问题
- 点击 Island 或使用会话列表快捷键可直接打开完整列表；尚待处理的审批与问题仍保留在列表中
- 保留尚未回答的终端问题，避免较旧的 Codex 快照（包括中断快照）覆盖更新的待回答状态
- 从完成通知切换到手动浏览时，及时结束该通知并取消自动关闭计时

## 改进

- 优化各内置主题在浅色和深色外观下的文字、面板与会话卡片对比度；悬浮任务数与宠物图形保持间距，避免重叠（#324）

- 版本 `0.33.0`，构建号 `85`；更新范围为 `v0.32.1...v0.33.0`

## Highlights

- Adds an independent appearance setting under Settings → General → Appearance: Follow System, Light, or Dark. Settings and floating panels use the selected appearance, which persists across restarts independently of experience themes and sound settings (#324)

## Fixes

- Fixes unreadable expanded tool results in light appearance, including code, shell output, filenames, line numbers, todos, and raw results; diff additions and deletions also use colors suited to light backgrounds
- Fixes a stale green bell and attention card blocking session access after a Codex session resumes or completes
- Clicking Island or using the session-list shortcut opens the full list, with pending approval and question controls retained
- Preserves unanswered terminal questions against older Codex snapshots, including interruption snapshots
- Ends the active completion notification and cancels its dismissal timer when switching to manual browsing

## Improvements

- Improves text, panel, and session-card contrast across all built-in themes in light and dark appearances, and keeps the floating task count clear of mascot artwork (#324)

## Notes

- App version `0.33.0`, build `85`; covers `v0.32.1...v0.33.0`
- The docked notch retains its classic black surface and dark content regardless of the appearance setting
- Use the DMG for manual installation; ZIP, Sparkle updates, and Linux x86_64 / aarch64 remote bridge packages are also provided
- [Full changelog](https://github.com/erha19/ping-island/compare/v0.32.1...v0.33.0)
